Japanese Onigiri × Indonesian Nasi Padang

Welcome to ONINASPAD

ONINASPAD is a fusion food that combines the convenience of Japanese onigiri with the bold taste of Indonesian Nasi Padang. It is a rice ball wrapped in nori (seaweed), filled with rendang, ayam gulai, or paru.

Portable. Affordable. Delicious. Perfect for students, workers, and anyone on the go.

Our Product

ONINASPAD looks like a classic Japanese onigiri. Inside, it hides authentic Padang fillings that bring a rich and spicy Indonesian taste.

Each piece weighs about 120 grams. It is wrapped in a peel-off plastic film to keep it fresh and clean.

How to Eat ONINASPAD

Eating ONINASPAD is quick and easy. Follow these simple steps:

1. Order

Buy from the store or through an app.

2. Pick Up

Take it from the refrigerated shelf.

3. Peel

Pull the plastic film tab to open.

4. Hold

Hold the nori to keep hands clean.

5. Eat

Bite in and enjoy the fusion.
